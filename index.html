Individual Values (IVs)

IVs are hidden values generated at the time when a Pokémon is caught, hatched, or received.

Actual values range from 0 to 31. Since IVs are randomly generated, these values are meant to be used in calculating your Pokémon's actual IVs.

Effort Values (EVs)

EVs are hidden values that a Pokémon species gives to your team when defeated in battle.

A Pokémon species can yield up to 3 EVs per stat.
